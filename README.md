## 🌟 Project Overview
**BioVerse 360°** is an interactive, pictorial science exhibition project designed for 13-year-old middle school science students. Rather than relying on static paragraphs and dry encyclopedia pages, this project brings human biology alive through:

1. **The Holographic 3D Body Scanner**:
   - Interactive anatomical layers (Full Anatomy, Circulatory, Respiratory, Nervous, Digestive, Skeletal).
   - Clickable organ hotspots with real-time biological telemetry, superpowers, and synchronized physiological stats.
2. **The Visual Systems Atlas (The Big 5)**:
   - **Circulatory System**: The Infinite 60,000-mile vascular highway and the SA node pacemaker pump.
   - **Respiratory System**: 600 million alveoli delivering oxygen across a tennis-court-sized surface area.
   - **Nervous System**: 86 billion neurons firing signals at up to 268 mph.
   - **Digestive System**: The 24-hour food journey and hydrochloric acid refinery.
   - **Skeletal & Muscular System**: 206 living bones stronger than concrete ounce-for-ounce and 600+ contracting muscles.
3. **Interactive Hands-On Mini Labs**:
   - **Lab 01: Cardiac ECG Waveform & Pulse Simulator**: Real-time canvas waveform with adjustable BPM (Sleep, Resting, Jog, Sprint) and synthesized heartbeat audio!
   - **Lab 02: Neural Reflex Speed Tester**: Millisecond-precision visual reflex reaction test with immediate human reaction feedback.
   - **Lab 03: Lung Expansion & Breath Pacer**: Synchronized 4-4-4 diaphragmatic breathing sphere.
   - **Lab 04: The 24-Hour Digestion Journey**: Step-by-step interactive timeline.
4. **The Junior Anatomist Challenge Quiz**:
   - 5 visual multiple-choice questions with instant sound cues, explanations, and an exhibition completion certificate!
5. **Mobile QR Companion**:
   - Scan directly with any smartphone to open the project live at the science fair table.
