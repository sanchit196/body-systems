## 🎒 Project Overview
**The Living Machine** is an interactive, visual middle school science exhibition project designed by a 13-year-old student. Rather than walls of dry textbook paragraphs or artificial AI renders, this project features **clean, human-designed educational vector diagrams, interactive organ explorers, and hands-on laboratory simulators**:

1. **🔬 The Interactive Body Explorer**:
   - Interactive organ switcher (Heart, Brain, Lungs, Stomach, Kidneys, Skeleton).
   - Clear, clean vector anatomical diagrams with labelled chambers, lobes, and vessels.
   - Student-friendly explanations, key workloads, and cool biological superpowers!
2. **📚 The Big 5 Systems Study Cards**:
   - **Circulatory System**: The 60,000-mile vascular network and the SA node pacemaker.
   - **Respiratory System**: 600 million alveoli with the surface area of a tennis court.
   - **Nervous System**: 86 billion neurons firing impulses at up to 268 mph.
   - **Digestive System**: The 30-foot food refinery and hydrochloric acid bath.
   - **Skeletal & Muscular System**: 206 living bones stronger than concrete ounce-for-ounce and 600+ contracting muscles.
3. **⚡ Middle School Interactive Labs**:
   - **Lab 01: Cardiac Pulse & ECG Simulator**: Real-time canvas waveform with adjustable BPM slider (Sleeping, Resting, Brisk Walk, Gym Sprint) and synthesized heartbeat audio!
   - **Lab 02: Nervous Reaction Time Tester**: Millisecond-precision visual reflex reaction test.
   - **Lab 03: Lung Expansion Breathing Pacer**: 4-4-4 diaphragmatic breathing cycle.
   - **Lab 04: The 24-Hour Food Journey**: Step-by-step digestion timeline.
4. **🎯 Pop Quiz & Certificate of Achievement**:
   - 5 visual multiple-choice questions with audio cues, explanations, and an exhibition certificate.
5. **📱 Mobile Companion QR Code**:
   - Built-in QR Code that classmates, teachers, and science fair visitors can scan directly from a phone or poster board.
