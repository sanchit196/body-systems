The Unstoppable Bio-Pump

WORKLOAD ~100,000 beats/day
POWER OUTPUT 7,500 L Blood / Day
SPEED 60 - 100 BPM
SIZE About your fist

🌟 Biological Superpower:

Visual Anatomy Atlas

The Big 5 Body Systems

Deep dive into each biological network with rich pictorial breakdowns and high-tech telemetry!

Circulatory System
🫀 Primary: Heart & Blood 🛣️ 60,000 Miles of Vessels

The Infinite Highway Network

The circulatory system delivers oxygen and nutrients to trillions of cells while carting away metabolic waste. Blood travels continuously through arteries, veins, and microscopic capillaries.

❤️ 4 Chambers 🩸 Red & White Cells ⚡ Electrical Pacemaker
Respiratory System
🫁 Primary: Lungs & Trachea 🎈 600 Million Alveoli

The Oxygen Exchange Engine

Every breath draws oxygen deep into hundreds of millions of microscopic air sacs called alveoli. Here, oxygen diffuses into the bloodstream in milliseconds while carbon dioxide is exhaled.

🎾 Surface Area of a Tennis Court 💨 12-20 Breaths/min 🫧 Alveoli Clusters
Nervous System
🧠 Primary: Brain & Spinal Cord ⚡ 268 MPH Signal Speed

The Biological Supercomputer

Your nervous system processes sensory inputs, calculates immediate responses, and commands voluntary movements through 86 billion neurons firing electrical synapses constantly.

⚡ 100 Trillion Synaptic Connections ⚡ 20 Watts Power 🕹️ Instant Reflexes
Digestive System
🥪 Primary: Stomach & Intestines ⏱️ 24-72 Hour Journey

The Nutrient Chemical Refinery

Transforming your lunch into pure cellular fuel. Stomach acids and digestive enzymes break down carbohydrates, proteins, and fats so the small intestine can absorb essential nutrients.

🧪 Stomach Acid pH 1.5 - 2.0 📏 22-foot Small Intestine 🦠 Trillions of Microbes
Skeletal & Muscular
🦴 206 Bones 💪 600+ Contracting Muscles

The Living Armor & Chassis

Bones are living tissue stronger than reinforced concrete per ounce, housing bone marrow that produces red blood cells. Muscles work in opposing pairs to pull, run, jump, and smile!

🛡️ Skull Shields Brain 🦴 Femur: Strongest Bone ⚡ Calcium Reserve
