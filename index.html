CIRCULATORY SYSTEM

The Human Heart

The Body's Tireless Power Pump

Workload: 100,000 beats/day
Pumping Power: 2,000 gallons of blood
Average Size: Same size as your fist!

⚙️ How It Works:

The heart is a hollow muscular organ with four chambers. The right side receives oxygen-used blood from the body and sends it to the lungs. The left side takes fresh, oxygen-rich blood from the lungs and pumps it through arteries to nourish every cell!

🌟 Cool Superpower:

The heart makes its own electrical pulses using a tiny cluster of cells called the SA Node! Because it has its own electrical system, it can keep beating even outside the body as long as it has oxygen!

The Big 5 Body Systems

Clean, pictorial cards illustrating each organ system with anatomical functions and key diagrams.

🫀 Circulatory System

The Body's Delivery Highway

Your blood travels through roughly 60,000 miles of vessels (arteries, veins, and microscopic capillaries). If unraveled, they would circle the Earth more than twice!

  • Arteries: Carry oxygen-filled red blood away from heart.
  • Veins: Bring oxygen-depleted blood back to the heart.
  • Capillaries: Tiny vessels where nutrients cross into cells.
🫁 Respiratory System

The Oxygen Gas Station

Every minute, your lungs inhale oxygen for your cells and exhale waste carbon dioxide through over 600 million microscopic air sacs called alveoli.

  • Trachea: The sturdy windpipe protected by cartilage rings.
  • Alveoli: Tiny clusters that have the surface of a tennis court!
  • Diaphragm: The dome muscle that pulls air into your chest.
🧠 Nervous System

The Master Electric Supercomputer

Your brain and spinal cord form the Central Nervous System. Neurons send lightning-fast electrical signals at speeds up to 268 miles per hour!

  • Cerebrum: Controls thinking, memory, speech, and senses.
  • Cerebellum: Manages balance, posture, and coordination.
  • Brainstem: Automatically handles breathing and heart rate.
🥪 Digestive System

The 30-Foot Food Refinery

Your digestive tract breaks down pizza, apples, and milk into molecular nutrients (proteins, carbohydrates, fats, vitamins) that your cells burn for fuel.

  • Stomach: Uses strong hydrochloric acid (pH 1.5) to digest food.
  • Small Intestine: 22-foot tube where 90% of nutrients are absorbed.
  • Large Intestine: Recovers water and compacts waste.
🦴 Skeletal & Muscular

Living Armor & Motor System

Bones are living tissue made of collagen and calcium. Ounce for ounce, human bone is stronger than steel and reinforced concrete!

  • Bone Marrow: Manufactures 2 million fresh red blood cells every second.
  • Pairs of Muscles: Biceps pull up; triceps pull down (opposing pairs).
  • Joints: Hinges and ball-and-sockets that let you run and throw.

LAB 04

🥪 The 24-Hour Food Journey Timeline

Click each stage to see what happens to an apple or sandwich as it travels through your digestive system:

1
Mouth & Teeth (1 min)

Chewing crushes food; salivary enzymes start breaking down carbohydrates.

2
Esophagus (5 sec)

Muscular waves (peristalsis) squeeze the food bolus down into your stomach.

3
Stomach Acid Bath (2-4 hrs)

Strong hydrochloric acid and pepsin liquefy food into a creamy soup called chyme.

4
Small Intestine (3-5 hrs)

Millions of tiny villi absorb 90% of all vitamins, minerals, proteins, and sugars.

5
Large Intestine (10-20 hrs)

Water is reclaimed, beneficial gut bacteria make vitamins, and waste is prepared.
